About the Artist

Lord
Saliba

Lord Saliba is a Knoxville-based bass music artist blending Dubstep, Trap, and Future Bass with the energy of underground club culture.

Inspired by the brostep movement of the early 2010s, his sound combines heavy low-end with cinematic drops and dance-focused remixing — designed to connect both bass fans and mainstream nightlife.

Starting out DJing living rooms before earning local bookings around Knoxville, Lord Saliba eventually transitioned into music production with Ableton, focusing on creating high energy flips and club-ready edits that feel equally at home in festival sets or late-night dancefloors.

Drawing inspiration from artists like Zeds Dead and the modern underground bass scene, his goal is simple: create Dubstep that feels powerful, immersive, and accessible while bringing more people into bass music culture.

"I want to make dubstep that's palatable for a general audience while still hitting hard."

— Lord Saliba
