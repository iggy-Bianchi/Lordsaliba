Biography

About
the Artist

Lord Saliba is a DJ and producer built for rooms that demand presence. His sound is precise, cinematic, and uncompromising. Every set is constructed like a score — layered with intention, built for impact.

From underground floors to festival stages, the name carries weight. Drawing from hip-hop, electronic, and pop influences, Lord Saliba crafts edits and originals that bridge the gap between the DJ booth and the mainstream.

His flips and remixes have gained traction across social platforms, earning him a reputation as a producer with a sharp ear for what moves a crowd. This is not background music. This is a statement.
