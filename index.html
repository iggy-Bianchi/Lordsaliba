About the Lord

Knoxville Based
Bass Music Artist

Forged in chaos. Built from darkness. Driven by purpose. Lord Saliba is more than an artist — he is a force from another realm. A ruler of sound, emotion, and energy. Creating a kingdom where music unites, liberates, and transforms.

Blending Dubstep, Trap, and Future Bass with the energy of underground club culture, his sound combines heavy low-end with cinematic drops and dance-focused remixing designed to connect both bass fans and mainstream nightlife.
